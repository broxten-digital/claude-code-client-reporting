## Rules for how to reason

Start with these four. They are wrong-answer-proof across every account.

- **Do not size an opportunity by applying the best segment's rate to the worst
  segment.** Different intent converts differently. If you quote an upside
  number, say what rate you assumed and why it is reachable.
- **A metric moving is not a finding.** State what changed, why, and whether it
  was the intended outcome.
- **Say when you do not know.** A stated unknown is more useful than a confident
  guess, and the guess is what destroys trust when someone checks.
- **A week is not a trend.** Neither is nine days.

## How to spot a tracking break instead of a performance drop

If sessions, clicks and impressions all held while conversions fell off a cliff,
that is tracking. A real demand drop moves traffic with it. When conversions
move alone, suspect measurement before you suspect performance.
