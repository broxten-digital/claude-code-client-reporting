Findings

01 · GSC + GA4

Emergency demand is up and the page cannot hold it

Impressions grew 3,429 to 4,821 and position improved to 4.8 on its own. The page keeps 19% of 10,991 organic sessions and converts 0.76%. Fix the page before adding budget.

02 · Ads + context

The implant line is working and should be reported separately

$10,246 produced 25 consults at $408 each, about 3.3x expected revenue in month one. Judging it on blended cost would end the best campaign in the account.

03 · Ads + GSC

Brand spend overlaps a first-place organic listing

$5,827 a month on brand terms that already rank 1.2 organically. Do not cut it blind. Run a three week geo holdout and measure the incremental booking.

Not comparable this period

Read these before comparing any month to another

  • July 14 to 22, form conversions under-recorded. The vendor changed and the tag stopped firing. Traffic held on all three platforms while conversions fell 43%. July's clean day run rate is about 884 bookings, not the 770 reported.
  • July ran a $99 new patient special on non-brand cleaning campaigns. August is not a decline against July. August is June again and July was promoted.
  • August 1, implant line launched. Any blended August number compares a mixed account to a single-service one.
  • Back to school. Elective demand softens 15 to 20% in the last two weeks of August. Emergency demand does not move.
